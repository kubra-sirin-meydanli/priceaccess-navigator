PriceAccess Navigator

AI-augmented decision support for pharmaceutical pricing & market access — Kubra Meydanli

The problem

Pricing and market access teams work in silos — without shared visibility into how a single price change in one market cascades across reference pricing baskets and downstream access outcomes. Most mid-sized pharma companies still manage this in Excel, with no probabilistic scenario modelling.

Solution approach (MVP)

Core use cases

UC1Mandatory price change cascade — a regulated cut in one market (AMNOG, CEPS, CIPM) recalculated across every market that references it. Risk prevention · compliance assurance
UC2Launch sequence optimisation — test launch order and lead price as reference baskets fill market by market, before launch. Revenue uplift · decision optimisation
UC3HTA outcome stress test — model a restricted or negative HTA outcome on revenue and adjacent markets. Risk prevention · HTA negotiation readiness

AI integration (Claude API)

IRP rule parsing — country rules in plain English, converted to executable logic at runtime

Narrative generation — cascade results turned into summaries ready for leadership meetings

Natural language input — type a free-form scenario, receive structured parameters back
