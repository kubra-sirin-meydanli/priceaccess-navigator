PriceAccess Navigator

AI augmented decision support for pharmaceutical pricing & market access · Kubra Meydanli

The problem

Pricing and market access teams work in silos, without shared visibility into how a single price change in one market cascades across reference pricing baskets and downstream access outcomes. Most pharma companies still manage this in Excel, with no probabilistic scenario modelling.

Solution approach (MVP)

Core use cases

UC1Mandatory price change cascade Demo focus: a regulated price change in one market (AMNOG, CEPS, CIPM), recalculated across every market that references it.
UC2Launch sequence optimisation: test launch order and lead price as reference baskets fill market by market, before launch.
UC3HTA outcome stress test: model a restricted or negative HTA outcome on revenue and adjacent markets.
